Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about PDF-to-Word conversion, privacy, OCR and document formatting.

Is the PDF to Word converter free?

Yes. The converter is free and does not require account registration.

Do I need an account?

No. Select, convert and download your file without creating an account.

Does PDFOrbit upload or save my files?

No. Conversion runs locally in your browser, so your PDF and generated DOCX are not sent to PDFOrbit.

Can scanned PDFs be converted?

Not directly. This converter extracts existing selectable text; image-only scans require a separate OCR step first.

Will formatting stay exactly the same?

Not always. Complex layouts, fonts, tables and scans can require cleanup after conversion.

What format should the download use?

The converter downloads a DOCX file containing the selectable text found in the PDF.
