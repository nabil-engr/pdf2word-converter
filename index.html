FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the most important questions about conversion and file handling.

Is this PDF to Word converter free?

Yes. Conversion is free and does not require an account.

Do you upload or keep my PDF?

No. The converter processes your PDF locally in your browser. The file is not sent to PDFOrbit.

Do I need to install software?

No. The conversion flow is browser based, so users can access it from a modern desktop or mobile browser.

What Word format will I get?

You will receive a DOCX file containing the selectable text found in the PDF.

Will the Word file look exactly like the PDF?

Not always. The converter prioritizes editable text. Complex layouts, tables, columns, images, and uncommon fonts may need adjustment. Image-only scanned PDFs require OCR, which this converter does not perform.
