Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about PDF-to-Word conversion, privacy, OCR and document formatting.

Is the PDF to Word converter free?

The core converter can be offered free without account registration.

Do I need an account?

No. The intended flow lets users upload, convert and download without creating an account.

Are uploaded files saved permanently?

The recommended production design is temporary processing only, with automatic cleanup after conversion or expiry.

Can scanned PDFs be converted?

Yes, but scanned files generally require OCR before their text can become editable.

Will formatting stay exactly the same?

Not always. Complex layouts, fonts, tables and scans can require cleanup after conversion.

What format should the download use?

DOCX is the recommended modern Word format for editable output.
