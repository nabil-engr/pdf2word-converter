PDF vs DOCX: which format should you use?

A simple comparison of PDF and DOCX for editing, sharing, collaboration, printing and layout consistency.

PDF and DOCX are both common document formats, but they solve different problems. Choosing the right one depends on whether you care more about fixed layout or easy editing.

Use PDF when

  • You want the document to look consistent across devices.
  • The file is ready to share, print or archive.
  • You do not want recipients casually editing the content.

Use DOCX when

  • You need to edit text frequently.
  • Several people are collaborating on the same document.
  • You want to reuse content, styles, tables or headings.

When conversion makes sense

Converting PDF to DOCX is useful when a fixed document needs to become editable again. The quality of the result depends on how the original PDF was created.
