How to edit a PDF in Microsoft Word

Learn what happens when Word opens a PDF and when converting the file first can give a cleaner editing experience.

Microsoft Word can open many PDF files and attempt to reconstruct them as editable documents. This works best with mostly text-based PDFs.

What Word does

Word converts the PDF into an editable document structure. It tries to preserve paragraphs, images, headings and other page elements.

When the result may change

Complex page layouts, forms, graphics, multi-column pages and unusual fonts may not look exactly the same after conversion.

Better workflow for frequent editing

  1. Convert the PDF to DOCX.
  2. Open the DOCX in Word.
  3. Review page breaks, tables and images.
  4. Save the cleaned document as your editable source.
