How to convert scanned PDF to Word with OCR

Understand how OCR turns scanned PDF pages into editable Word text and what improves recognition quality.

A scanned PDF often contains page images rather than selectable text. To turn those pages into editable Word content, the converter needs optical character recognition (OCR).

How to tell if a PDF is scanned

Try selecting a sentence in the PDF viewer. If the entire page behaves like one image, the file may be image-only and require OCR.

What improves OCR accuracy

  • Sharp, high-resolution scans.
  • Pages that are straight rather than skewed.
  • Good contrast between text and background.
  • Correct language detection.
  • Minimal handwriting or decorative fonts.

What OCR can struggle with

Handwriting, faint photocopies, dense tables, stamps, unusual fonts and heavily compressed scans can reduce recognition quality.

Important: OCR creates editable text, but the final layout may still need manual cleanup in Word.
