Privacy Policy

How the browser-based converter handles documents and related technical information.

Last updated: August 19, 2026.

Your PDF files

PDFOrbit processes PDF files locally in your web browser. The converter does not upload your PDF or the generated DOCX file to PDFOrbit servers. Closing or refreshing the page clears the converter's active in-memory state.

Account data

The converter does not require an account and PDFOrbit does not maintain user profiles.

Technical data

The website's hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and security logs. That information is governed by the hosting provider's policies and is not used by the converter to inspect document contents.

Analytics and cookies

PDFOrbit currently includes no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or application cookies. If that changes, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before those tools are enabled.

Third-party support

Support requests are handled through GitHub Issues. Information submitted there is sent to GitHub and is normally public. Do not include PDF files, passwords, personal data or confidential information in an issue.

Contact

For a general privacy question, use the contact page without including sensitive information.
