How to handle sensitive PDFs safely

A practical privacy checklist for converting PDFs that may contain confidential, personal or business information.

Before uploading a sensitive PDF to any online service, understand how the service handles files, how long temporary copies exist and whether documents are used for any purpose beyond processing.

Before uploading

  • Remove information that is not needed for the conversion.
  • Check the service's privacy policy and file-retention statement.
  • Avoid public or shared computers for confidential documents.
  • Use a trusted network connection.

What a privacy-focused converter should do

A strong design uses temporary processing, automatic cleanup, transport encryption, restricted server access and clear retention rules.

Site implementation note: Only claim automatic deletion if the backend actually enforces it.

After conversion

Download the result, verify it, and delete local temporary copies if the document is sensitive and you no longer need them.
