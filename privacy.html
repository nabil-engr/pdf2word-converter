Privacy Policy

A simple privacy-policy starter for a conversion service that does not keep a permanent user document library.

Important: This is starter website copy, not legal advice. Update it to match your actual hosting, analytics, logging, retention and third-party services before launch.

Files you upload

Uploaded documents should be processed only for the requested conversion. The production system should use temporary storage and remove processing files automatically after the conversion finishes or expires.

Account data

The core service is designed without required user accounts, so no account profile is needed for standard conversion.

Technical data

Servers may process standard technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP address, browser information, request timestamps and security logs. Define an appropriate retention period in production.

Analytics and cookies

If analytics or advertising tools are added later, disclose them here and provide any consent controls required for the regions you serve.

Contact

Publish a real support or privacy contact address before launch.
